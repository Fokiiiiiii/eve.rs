# ADR 0017: The cynosural field is a sim-timed deployable; the cycle's end lights it (protocol 41)

- Status: accepted (track B7, 2026-10-09)
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Relates to: ADR 0011 (area effects), ADR 0012 (deployed objects and one expiry owner), ADR 0013 (module work cycle wire)

## Context

Track B6c left the cynosural field generators without a body (WP19). The SDE gives the generators their numbers (activation duration 600000 ms for the
normal and industrial generators and 60000 ms for the covert one; fuel 500, 50 and 800 per cycle; the speed ceiling 1334 = 500 m/s) and the field
types their lifetime (explosionDelay 281 = 600000 ms on 21094, 28650 and 52696). The EVE University wiki gives the lighting rules (a fleet member, not in
high-security space, the activation time before the field is lit). ADR 0012 asks that a timed object such as a cyno field is a persisted `Deployed`
record expired by the sweep. The simulation path that exists for bombs and probes (ADR 0012 addendum) is a sim-side timer that a restart drops.

## Decision

1. **A new module action, tag 27** (`SimAction.CynoField`, `Action::CynoField`): as the generator's cycle ends, the field is released as a deployable
   where the ship is, with the field type's ball and its lifetime. The field is announced with `AddBalls2` and removed with `RemoveBalls` when its lifetime
   is up (the simulation's `advance_deployables`). A field does not explode.
2. **Lighting is at the cycle's end.** The wiki's "activation time" is the time before the field is lit, so the cycle is the activation and the field
   appears when it ends. EveJS (`cynosuralField.js`) spawns the field at activation instead; the difference is recorded in DEVIATIONS.
3. **The generator is one cycle.** It lights its field once and stops (repeat 1); the fuel of that cycle is taken before it starts, through the shared
   mechanism (`BurnsFuel`, set by `ShipEffect` from the consumption type).
4. **Protocol 41.** Tag 27 is the only wire change; `SimCodec.ProtocolVersion` and `PROTOCOL_VERSION` (`crates/eve-space/src/link/mod.rs`) move from 40
   to 41 together. The golden messages are regenerated by `tools/dev/gen-simlink-fixtures.py` (two new entries only).
5. **Known limitation, to be removed.** The field is a sim-timed deployable, as the bombs and probes are, so a restart drops it. ADR 0012 asks for a
   persisted `Deployed` record expired by the sweep; that is not built in this track (open, DEVIATIONS "Module rules, track B7").

## Consequences

- Tests: `deployable::tests::a_cynosural_field_is_lit_where_the_ship_is_as_its_activation_ends_and_is_taken_out_when_its_lifetime_is_up`,
  the golden entry in `oracle/fixtures/simlink/messages.json`, and `CynoFieldHostTests` (the refusals of the fleet and of high-security space).
- Not built: the jump-in restrictions (the eve.rs simulation has no capital jump drive), the Mobile Cynosural Inhibitor, the Cynosural System Jammer
  (E-03), and the fleet beacon state that EveJS sets when a field is lit.
