# Probe scanning, directional scan, bookmarks, and signatures: deep-fix handoff

## Scope and result

This increment traced the player-facing scan, signature tracker, and bookmark paths against the whole V24.01 client and EveJS 261028.1. It fixed one state-corruption defect in probe scanning: a non-finite coordinate in a client probe patch could reach the durable scan job payload, make JSON serialization fail, and leave the scan unfinished. The route now rejects that vector and uses the probe's current destination.

No protocol, scan formulas, static data, bookmark behavior, or notification order changed. The existing detailed audits remain useful for the full method inventories: `DEEPCHECK_scanMgr.md` and `DEEPCHECK_bookmarkMgr.md` in this directory.

## Root cause and change

- Host dispatch reaches `SpaceProbeScans.RequestScansAsync` from the bound `scanMgr.RequestScans` route (`dotnet/src/Eve.Services.Space/SpaceProbeScans.cs:93,251`). `ReadProbeRequests` parses a probe's destination through `Vector3`; previously it only checked numeric type and vector length, so NaN / infinity survived parsing. The updated `Vector3` returns no patch for a non-finite coordinate (`SpaceProbeScans.cs:543-554`). The request then keeps the persisted destination rather than moving the probe to invalid space (`:267-287`) and only schedules a serializable scan job (`:300-313`).
- The failure was reproduced before the fix in `ScanProbeHostTests.A_probe_scan_ignores_non_finite_client_destinations_instead_of_persisting_them`: the request failed in `DurableScheduler.ScheduleAsync` because its JSON payload contained NaN. After the fix, the DB-backed simulation test verifies that the scan reaches `OnSystemScanStopped` and the stored probe coordinates remain finite and unchanged (`dotnet/tests/Eve.Services.Space.Tests/ScanProbeHostTests.cs`, same test).
- EveJS 261028.1 normalizes probe patches in `/home/user/evejs-261028.1/src/server/src/services/exploration/probes/probeScanRuntime.js:330-372`; its `clonePosition` converts non-finite components to the probe position fallback (`:105-146`). This change follows that observable fallback. EveJS runs the scan at `/home/user/evejs-261028.1/src/server/src/services/exploration/scanMgrService.js:2602-2801`.
- The probe range, strength and maximum-deviation checks remain grounded in the EVE University [Probe Scanning](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Probe_scanning) mechanics and the pinned SDE attributes in `ProbeScanMath.cs:10-54`. Directional range remains bounded by SDE attribute 5796 with the 14.3 AU fallback in `dotnet/src/Eve.Services.Space/Scanning/SpaceDirectionalScans.cs:15-29`, consistent with the EVE University [Directional Scanning](https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Directional_scanning) range description. No mechanics deviation was needed.
- Signature full-state and delta ordering remains: the initial full-state callback is sent after registration, followed by later signature deltas from the tracker provider; the Host wire regression is `dotnet/tests/Eve.Host.Tests/ScanMgrHostWireTests.cs:13-111`. The scan stop notification remains after the scheduler reads target state and records warpable scan results (`SpaceProbeScans.cs:364-394`).
- Bookmark mutations remain on the shared PostgreSQL BookmarkStore and their notification ordering remains as detailed by `DEEPCHECK_bookmarkMgr.md`; EveJS 261028.1 has the direct methods in `/home/user/evejs-261028.1/src/server/src/services/character/accessGroupBookmarkMgrService.js` and the live bookmark methods in `.../services/ship/beyonceService.js`. No new bookmark defect was evidenced in this increment.
