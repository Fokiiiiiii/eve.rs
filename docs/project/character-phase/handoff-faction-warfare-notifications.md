## Implementation

- Direct enlistment validates an occupier faction, corporate allowed-faction policy, and unmodified standing >= 0 before persistence. The persisted corporation store already checks the Director role when setting allowed enlistment factions (`dotnet/src/Eve.Corporations/CorporationStore.FactionWarfare.cs:5-20`); no duplicate role check was added at the RPC layer.
- Character join now moves the character to the faction's militia NPC corporation, clears alliance session state, persists faction/cooldown, changes the session, and queues `OnEnlistmentStateUpdated(factionID, directFactionID, corpFactionID)` after the RPC response.
- Direct enlist and leave queue that same notification after persistence/session changes. LP pool updates are emitted after transaction commit and sent to same-system sessions; the donor receives its notification after its own response, other clients immediately.
- No database was used for this task.

### Implementation

- Direct character faction state and `RelationKinds.FactionMember` now commit in the same character/relation transaction. Reconciliation uses the persisted character state at login/session refresh; direct faction state takes precedence over corporation, then alliance faction state.
- Faction-warfare corporation and alliance relation projection runs in the existing FW state transaction, including due downtime changes. Crimewatch refreshes the current character/corporation/alliance state before evaluating the relation chain.
- Legal militia targeting treats opposing occupier factions as enemies, including opposite direct enlistees in one corporation. Friendly occupiers and non-militia factions remain non-legal. This follows EVE University’s Direct Enlistment and legal Militia War Targets rules over EveJS 261028.1 `server/src/services/corporation/warRuntimeState.js:1607-1629`; the rationale and tested behavior are recorded in `docs/reference/compat/DEVIATIONS.md`.
- `GetMyEnlistment` retains its three-value list and `OnEnlistmentStateUpdated` its three arguments.
