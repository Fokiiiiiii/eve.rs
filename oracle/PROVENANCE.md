# Oracle provenance

The golden fixtures and the JS benchmark baseline in this directory are
**generated from the EVE.js reference implementation** and copied here. The
generators stay in the EVE.js repository (they `require` its modules), so this
repository only carries their output.

## Scenarios recorded from the running reference

`fixtures/scenarios/` are not generated by the JavaScript generators: they are what the running EVE.js reference
answered to the headless client (`tools/headless-client/scripts/record_character_scenarios.py`, against a reference
started by `tools/dev/ref-server.sh` on data built from SDE build 3396210). `referenceCommit` and `recordedAt` are in
each file. They pin what the client can see on the way from login to a session with a character; where eve.rs
deliberately differs, `docs/compat/DEVIATIONS.md` says so. `login_to_character.json` is the walk, `starters.json` what
each bloodline is given, and `skills.json` what the skill handler answers for a new character of each race (the three
calls that return the whole skill list share one copy, marked `{"$same": "GetSkills"}`), `character_info.json` what
`charMgr` and `account` answer for a new character of each race, and `stations.json` the reference's station record for
a sample of 62 stations (every 87th of the static data and two more), which the station names are checked against, and
`inventory.json` what the inventory broker and the bound inventories of a new character's hangar, station and ship
answer, and `dogma.json` the answer of `dogmaIM.GetAllInfo` (ship, fitted modules, character, brain, state) for a new
character of each of the four playable races (with the three notifications sent after it), which the dogma engine's values
and the brain are checked against, and `fitting.json` what the client is sent when a module is taken out of its slot, taken
offline and put online again (the events and the items that moved), and what the reference answers to a module call it
cannot do, and `training.json` the skill queue of a Caldari and an Amarr character (starting a skill, saving, reordering,
pausing and resuming a queue, the errors it gives, stopping, the history, each with the events sent and the skill window
afterwards), recorded at the reference's own training speed, and `training_fast.json` the levels that finish while the client
watches or is away, which needs a reference started with `EVEJS_SKILL_TRAINING_SPEED=60000` (the file records the speed it saw,
and the replay is skipped by a server that trains at another).
and `undock.json` a new character leaving its station, being sent the solar system, flying and docking again (the ship
service's `Undock`, the ballpark bound for the system and `UpdateStateRequest`, the movement commands and `CmdDock`; each step
with everything the client is sent, the destiny actions opened),
`movement.json` the orders a ship in space is given (`CmdAlignTo`, `CmdOrbit`, `CmdFollowBall`, `CmdStop`, `CmdSetSpeedFraction`, repeats, a
target that is not there) with what the reference answers and sends, and `approach.json` a ship that flies out of docking range and is told to
dock (the reference makes it follow the station, gives the user error `DockingApproach`, does not dock it by itself, and docks it when asked
again in range; the file also holds the ship's distance from the station's surface every few seconds of the approach). Each is recorded
with no other player in the system: another ship would appear in the balls.
`warp.json` a ship warping to a planet two AU away (the update that starts the warp, every event until the ship is at rest with the seconds each arrived
at, the ship's distance to the warp's end every three seconds, and the messages for a warp that cannot be done).
`jump.json` a ship warping to the stargate of its system (11.7 AU away) and jumping: the update that starts the warp, every event until it is at rest with
the seconds each arrived at, `CmdStargateJump` and everything the client is sent for twelve seconds (the effect and the stop, `OnJumpQueueUpdate`, the objects
let go, `OnItemChange`, `OnJumpCloakUpdated`, the session change), then the ballpark of the next system bound and its state asked for. The reference draws
where the ship lands and where it appears at random, so those are bounds, not values (`crates/eve-space/tests/jump_matches_reference.rs`).
`others.json` two characters in one system (a watcher in space and an actor that undocks, binds, is given orders, stops, docks, undocks again and warps to a
planet): for each step what the actor was sent and what the watcher was sent, with the seconds each arrived at. It shows what the reference tells a ship of
another that comes, moves, warps off and docks (the ball and its numbers in one update, the orders as they are, the ship taken out of view 7 seconds into a
warp, in a `PackagedAction`) and that the newcomer's first `AddBalls2` lists both ships.
`target.json` two characters in one system by the same station (the watcher is locked, the actor locks): what each is sent, with the seconds each arrived at, as the actor
asks for its targets and its targeters, locks itself, a planet and something that is not there (`DeniedTargetSelf`, `DeniedTargetingAttemptFailed`, `TargetingAttemptCancelled`),
locks a ship still under its undocking protection (`CustomNotify`), locks the watcher (about 3.8 s, `OnTarget add` and the damage state to both, `otheradd` to the watcher),
the station (0.76 s), locks again, unlocks (`lost`, `otherlost`), gives a lock up, unlocks with a list, clears (`clear`, `otherlost`), sees a locked ship dock (`lost` when it has
docked), warps with the station locked (`clear` when the warp is entered), tries to lock in warp (`DeniedTargetSelfWarping`) and from 2 AU away (`TargetNotWithinRangeGeneric`).
`module.json` two characters in one system (the actor works its modules, the watcher looks on): the actor flies at full speed, starts its afterburner (the ship's mass and top speed and the effect in two destiny updates, the module's and the ship's attributes, `OnGodmaShipEffect`, and every half second the capacitor charge as it recharges), starts it again (`EffectAlreadyActive2`), stops it (the module keeps on to the end of its cycle: 6 s later the attributes are given back, `OnGodmaShipEffect` says it is off and the destiny update takes the effect off), stops it again (a message), and tries the gun with no target, with a target and no charge (`SafetyActivated`: the reference's safety, not the charge) and the miner on a ship and with no target. Each step holds what the actor was sent and what the watcher was sent, with the seconds each arrived at.

`health.json` one character damaging and healing its ship (`/dmg` in the hangar is the reference's only way to hurt a ship on demand; in space it refuses, "Dock first…"): the docked ship asked for whole (`GetAllInfo`: the ship's damage as a fraction, its capacitor, shield and armor in points, and the modules' own numbers) as new, after a light `/dmg`, after a heavy one and after `/heal`; the events each command sends (`OnItemChange` and `OnCfgDataChanged` for the ship and its modules; a heal adds an `OnModuleAttributeChanges` for each hurt module); the undock with the ship damaged heavily (its shield and capacitor are full in space and its armor and hull are as they were: the damage state in the destiny stream is `((1.0, tau, time), 0.4, 0.65)`); the ship asked for in space; and a `/heal` in space (the ship's armor and hull, `OnDamageStateChange` to everyone who sees it, the capacitor and the modules). The last steps also hold what an NPC of the system did to the ship (`OnDamageMessage`, the shield charge every half second as it recharges): not compared, the evidence for the NPC slice. Each step holds the answer (or the error) and what the client was sent, with the seconds each arrived at.
`--only walk|starters|skills|info|stations|inventory|dogma|fitting|training|training_fast|undock|approach|movement|warp|jump|others|target|module` records one and leaves the others as they are.

`fixtures/simlink/messages.json` is not from the reference: it is what `tools/dev/gen-simlink-fixtures.py` writes, the golden
frames of the link between the service host and the simulation (`docs/adr/0007`), a third writer of the format that the Rust
simulation (`crates/eve-space/tests/simlink_fixtures.rs`) and the C# host replay.

`crates/eve-space/data/undock_points.json` is not a recording: it is the reference's table of where a ship comes out of each type of station
(its `stationGraphicLocators`, made from the client's graphics), reduced to the undock points by `tools/dev/extract-undock-points.py`. The SDE
does not have it. It is used as data; none of the reference's code is copied.

## Wreck types

`fixtures/wrecks/ship_wrecks.json`: the wreck the reference's `resolveShipWreckType` (`server/src/space/wreckUtils.js`) gives the published ships of the
static data and a sample of the NPC ships (424 types), made by `tools/dev/gen-ship-wrecks-fixture.js` (it loads the reference from `EVEJS`, see its
header). The Rust port `crates/eve-space/src/wreck_types.rs` (ADR 0009) is held to it by `crates/eve-space/tests/wreck_types_match_reference.rs`.

## NPC hull weapons

`fixtures/npc/hull_weapons.json`: what the reference's `npcEquipment` (`getNpcWeaponModules`, `buildNpcWeaponModuleSnapshot`: `server/src/space/npc/npcEquipment.js`,
`server/src/space/combat/weaponDogma.js`) makes of the weapon of every NPC ship type of the static data that has one (4446 of 4687 types) and of one in twelve of
those that have none, with the attributes and effects of the type that it worked from (the reference's `getTypeAttributeMap`, `getTypeDogmaEffects`), made by
`tools/dev/gen-npc-armament-fixture.js`. The C# port `dotnet/src/Eve.Npc/NpcArmament.cs` (ADR 0009) is held to it by `HullWeaponFixtureTests`. The simulation
knows one weapon to an NPC ship (its module is the ship), so 117 types that the reference gives two turrets (`targetAttack` and `targetDisintegratorAttack`) are
compared on the first, and the 18 types of the chain-lightning vorton projector have none.
