# Alpha queue cap accounting review

Date: 2026-11-07

## Decision

Keep the save-time Alpha cap refusal. The 5,000,000 SP check uses live trained SP and free points plus remaining queue SP; adding elapsed head SP to persisted total and subtracting it from the queue leaves the full increment counted once. V24.01's estimator starts with cached skill SP and accrues from queue start, while its panel total comes from cached `GetAllSkills`; the returned skill points can reflect queue-start or live state. The panel can therefore subtract elapsed progress from a total that already includes it, or from a queue-start baseline that omits it, and forecast within the cap while the authoritative save rejects. The client handles that refusal. eve.rs matches EveJS on live-head save accounting and includes free points per EVE University; EveJS's save helper omits free points.

## Evidence

The queue panel uses that cached total (`skillQueuePanelNew.py:412`) and subtracts estimated current-level SP from each line increment (`skillQueuePanelNew.py:409-428`; wrapper `skillQueueSvc.py:691-700`). ## Regression rationale

`CharacterTrainingTests.Saving_an_alpha_queue_over_the_live_cap_is_refused_even_when_the_panel_forecast_fits` sets persisted SP to 4,999,000 and the full queue increment to 1,165. It verifies that the panel's estimated current-level SP reduces the queue forecast enough to fit, while the live server still sees a positive over-cap total and refuses. The client sources show why the estimate may overlap progress in the total: it starts with the cached skill value and accrues from queue start, while `GetAllSkills` can return queue-start or live points. The test also accepts totals just below and at the cap, and verifies that `activate=false` pauses and rebases the active line.

`TrainingTests.Alpha_queues_refuse_levels_and_skill_points_beyond_the_clone_limits` also verifies that free SP is included in the cap (`dotnet/tests/Eve.Skills.Tests/TrainingTests.cs:49-65`).
