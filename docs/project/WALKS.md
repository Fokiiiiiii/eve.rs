A focused walk validates its own RPC sequence and persisted result; it does not imply that adjacent systems are joined into one play-through. `pytest.ini` enforces a 300-second timeout per test through `pytest-timeout`. Database-backed walks require the assigned lane's `EVE_PG_URL` and lock. Durations below are measured wall time where captured. Other entries record the observed live-run result and 300-second per-test bound; grouped times sum the named tests.
