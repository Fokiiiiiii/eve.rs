## 0. Why specs are not in the SDE/client
Server-authoritative architecture: dungeon wave triggers and NPC rosters, PI resource seeds / spherical-harmonic coefficients, contract/mission/war state transitions live only on the Tranquility cluster. Since 2022 the backend moved to "Quasar" (gRPC, event-driven microservices); e.g. Insurgency progress is computed by a Quasar Insurgency service subscribing to events from EVE Online and EVE Vanguard; the client only renders the status. So client V24.01 source and SDE cannot contain these tables.

## 1. Epic Arc "Vision of Greatness" (arc 132): four simulation missions (confidence: 高 from UniWiki, updated 2026-06..08)

Start agent: Cassandra Ahfrim, AIR Laboratories, Manifest V - AIR Laboratories Trade Center. Each simulation allows only that empire's Standard Frigates (non-navy T1 base frigates, 6 hulls); a Supplies container granted at accept time contains the hull, required modules and ammo. Arc completion: +2.5% standing with Amarr, Caldari, Gallente, Minmatar; no derived standing loss.

Pocket 1: 5 friendly Megacorp Transport Vessels escape while hostile NPCs attack them; jam the hostiles with ECM. Pocket 2: remote-shield-boost the transports and kill 10 hostile NPCs. Done when pocket 1 transports escaped and pocket 2's 10 hostiles are dead. Reward base 119,000 ISK, time bonus 123,000 ISK, total 242,000, LP 0.
- Battle of Pator: Minmatar Standard Frigates; kit: 1 Rifter, 1 Data Analyzer I, 1 125mm Gatling AutoCannon I, 1 1MN Afterburner I, 1 Target Painter I, 2,000 EMP S. - Golgothan Fields: Amarr Standard Frigates; kit: 1 Punisher, 1 Small Energy Neutralizer I, 1 Gatling Pulse Laser I, 1 1MN Afterburner I, 1 Small Remote Armor Repairer I, 3 Multifrequency S. Pocket 1: friendly dreadnought Nuras' Revelation (remote-repair it). Pocket 2: 4 hostile (Minmatar-side) NPCs. Pocket 3: enemy flagship dreadnought "Flagship Naglfar" (neutralize its capacitor). ## 3. Pirate Insurgency Corruption/Suppression (confidence 高, patch notes 2023-11-21.1, 2023-12-07.1, Version 21.06)
Introduced with the Havoc expansion (Nov 2023). Pirate militia (Angel Cartel Malakim Zealots / Guristas Commando Guri) complexes raise Corruption; empire militia raise Suppression. Backend: Quasar Insurgency service (gRPC, event driven) aggregates EVE and EVE Vanguard events. Progress: a 60-minute-respawn top-tier site (90 min at stage 5) gives 52,500 LP and +1.5% Corruption (or Suppression); Ice Heist (stage 3-4+, T1/T2/T3 battleships and below, up to 30 pilots share LP, Suspect timer for neutrals): escort a Guided Tanker (start within 5,000 m, stops beyond 10 km or when destroyed); first side to deliver 10 (10 points) gets progress.
Corruption stage effects (cumulative): S1 pirate PvP loot drops +7.5% total, no Faction Navy/Criminal Police NPCs in high-sec; S2 loot +15%, roaming pirate spawns attack all non-pirate players (also at stargates); S3 loot +22.5%, roaming pirates, Insurgency spreads to adjacent systems, Ice Heist appears; S4 loot +30%, stronger roaming pirates (not visiting stargates), pirate militia +2 AU/s warp speed; S5 loot +37.5%, lowsec sentry guns ignore criminal timers, Interdiction Sphere Launchers / Warp Disruption Field Generators / Bomb Launchers / Defender Launchers usable in lowsec, autopilot route icon becomes a triangle.
Suppression stage effects: S1 NPC bounty +5%, NPC-kill security status gain +5%; S2 +10% / +10%, empire FW militia LP +5%; S3 +15% / +15%, LP +10%, Corruption gain in that system -30%, roaming Enforcer NPCs that attack only pirate-militia players (stargates visited), Ice Heist; S4 +20% / +20%, LP +15%, Corruption gain -30%, stronger Enforcers (no stargate visits), empire-militia players get +10% Stasis Webifier and Warp Scrambler range; S5 +25% / +25%, LP +20%, Corruption gain -30%, strong Enforcers, much stronger sentry guns with ECM and Target Painting that are immune to Corruption stage-5 sentry-disable.
Rules: Corruption never decreases; if Suppression reaches stage 5 first, that system's Corruption is capped at stage 4 and counts as a win; the campaign ends when the number of stage-5 systems (either side) reaches the campaign cap. Version 21.06 added VFX/audio for Insurgency systems and pirate FOBs. Report's loot-bonus formula B_loot(S) = 0.075 x S (additive vs multiplicative on a 50% base drop chance is ambiguous: 68.75% vs 87.5%).
Not established in the supplied report: stage threshold values (points or percent), natural decay, the Public Gateway insurgency .proto names/fields.

## 4. Weapon grouping ("weapon bank") (confidence 高 from CCP dev blog/patch notes; Quantum Rise 1.0, 2008-11)
Grouping stacks same-type weapons (turrets/launchers) into one icon: one click starts/stops all, one click reloads all with a new ammo type; to put a grouped weapon offline the group must be broken first. Edge case reported 2019-2023: the weapon cycle timer can overrun when the killing cycle and the reload timer interfere (confidence 中). Not established in the supplied report: behaviour when ammo counts differ or one runs dry, capacitor-short partial start rules, whether overheating is per group or per module.

## 9. FW Battlefield and CRAB
Battlefield (UniWiki Faction warfare, 高): appears only in frontline systems, at most 2 at once, a new one spawns 3 hours after one completes. Three landing points (one per side, one for neutral capsuleers); neutrals land ~75-125 km from the capture points, enlisted pilots nearer. Several capture points on the grid; staying within 30 km of a point advances your side per tick; at 100% the battlefield completes. Enemy-faction NPCs or enemy militia players within 30 km pause capture; pods cannot capture or hold. Completion: up to 30 pilots on the winning side's grid get 150,000 LP (less each beyond 30), winning side +2,000 Victory Points, big system Advantage increase (Advantage also from Propaganda Broadcast Structures and Rendezvous Points; reduced by destroying enemy Supply Caches/Depots and Listening Outposts [Anchoring III, max 3 per system]; making a system vulnerable needs a 3,000 VP lead over defence). System upgrade cumulative LP: L1 40,000, L2 60,000, L3 90,000, L4 140,000, L5 200,000, buffer 300,000. Faction tier LP modifier: T1 (0-20%) -50%, T2 (21-40%) 0, T3 (41-60%) +75%, T4 (61-80%) +150%, T5 (81-100%) +225%. Not established in the supplied report: blocker NPC types/counts/period, frontline selection algorithm, objective archetype 67 internals, exact VP for PvP kills.
CRAB (Version 19.09 Oct 2021; Equinox: Megacyte cost -50%; Version 23.02 / 2026-03-18.1 Catalyst Update: no deployment inside asteroid belts, new Carrier-only variant): BPC (5-run) from CONCORD and DED LP stores for 20,000,000 ISK + 20,000 LP; 3 h 20 min per run; materials per run Tritanium 2,416,000, Pyerite 502,000, Isogen 310,400, Zydrine 2,840, Broadcast Node 2, Megacyte 420. Deploy: Anchoring III, low-sec and null-sec only, 20 s activation delay, 1 h lifetime, Shield/Armor/Structure 50,000 each, shows as a system-wide warpable beacon. Linking: Carrier, Dreadnought, Supercarrier, Titan (scanning can be done by subcapital fleets); the linking ship is held 4 minutes (240 s): tether blocked, cloak disrupted, warp disabled, +10% resistances; the four effects last 60 s more after linking (5 minutes total). Scanning: Rogue Drone NPCs spawn; while they are inside the scan perimeter scanning is blocked; 10 minutes (600 s) of uninterrupted scanning completes it (NPC AI remembers aggressors). Reward: a cargo of Rogue Drone Mutaplasmids (Durability, Firepower, Navigation, Projection); for the first 120 s only the linking ship can access it, then the CRAB self-destructs and drops everything as a wreck. Limits: CEQ (Complex Encryption Qubits) is per character, regenerates continuously with ~22 h to full, standard variant about 3 links per day (each ~7.3-8 h of CEQ); Signal Interference is per system (starts 0, rises per use, saturated = no linking, about 30-40 uses/day, decays over ~25 h, independent of downtime). Carrier variant: Carrier only, Rogue Drones do not attack drones/fighters, 16-minute (960 s) scan, rewards scale, 4 links/day (~5.5 h CEQ each), lower interference per use. Player-measured income (中): ~250M ISK average per site, 20-min bounty tick 80-140M, 300-400M bounty per site, drops 100M-1B, officer spawn 1-35B. Not established in the supplied report: integer CEQ and interference internals, wave-2 rosters, per-type bounties.

## Report's closing advice
Separate (a) SDE-importable static attributes (burst charge buff IDs/multipliers, Remote Sensing range) from (b) server dynamic state machines that must be authored (AIR arc pocket conditions, Insurgency stage cumulative effects and stage-5 lock, FW battlefield 30 km blocker logic, CRAB four phases with CEQ/interference timers). Externalise parameters whose values are not public (SH seeds, NPC wave timers) into configuration tables that can be calibrated later.
